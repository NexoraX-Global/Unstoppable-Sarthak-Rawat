RUNNER TRAINING BRIEFING

HOW TO SURVIVE THE RAILS

  • SWITCH LANES: Swipe Left/Right (or tap ◀ / ▶ or A/D keys) to weave across the 3 broad-gauge tracks.
  • HIGH-JUMP ONTO TRAINS & OVER BARRIERS: Swipe Up (or tap ▲ or W/Space) to leap high over barricades or land directly on top of WAP-7 & LHB train roofs!
  • SLIDE UNDER GANTRIES: Swipe Down (or tap ▼ or S/Down) to tuck and slide beneath high overhead signal boards, or fast-drop mid-air.
  • WATCH THE RPF INSPECTOR: Grazing a train or hitting a barrier alerts the uniformed officer behind you. If you crash or stumble when he is close, he will sprint up and apprehend you!
  • POWER-UPS:
    • Aero Thruster (Jetpack): Soar high above every train and collect aerial gold coin ribbons.
    • Flux Magnet: Pulls coins from all 3 lanes automatically.
    • Overdrive Surge: Boosts speed and doubles your score multiplier.
    • Kavach Shield: Absorbs one direct obstacle or train collision safely.
